🕊️ Privacy Policy — Protect Your Peace

Effective: November 4, 2026

Protect Your Peace is a free calming web app for all ages. This policy explains, in plain language, what data the app does and does not collect.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server that stores your data. Your progress — such as streaks, favorites, journal entries, and Peace Garden growth — is stored only on your own device (in your browser's local storage). If you clear your browser data or switch devices, that progress resets, because it never leaves your device.

The app's calming sounds are generated on your device. Nothing you type in the journal or gratitude sections is sent anywhere.

Advertising

Protect Your Peace displays ads through Google AdSense in designated areas of the app. Ads never appear in meditation areas or in the kids' corner. Google may use cookies to show ads based on your interests. You can learn how Google uses data and opt out of personalized ads in your Google ad settings.

Hosting

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard technical information (such as IP addresses in server logs) as described in GitHub's own privacy statement. We do not receive or use that information.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for all ages, including a kids' corner with games and coloring. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect no personal information from children either. No ads appear in the kids' corner.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
